# Customer-behaviour-analysis
## Project Objective
This project analyzes a retail consumer behavior dataset to identify trends, improve engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies. The goal is to help a leading retail company understand changes in purchasing patterns across demographics, product categories, and sales channels

## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/shrya4053/Customer-behaviour-analysis/blob/main/Screenshot%202026-01-09%20002234.png">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
1. Which gender contributes more to total revenue, and what is the revenue split between male and female customers?
2. Which customers used promo codes and still spent more than the overall average purchase amount, indicating high-value discount users?
3. Which products have the highest average customer satisfaction based on review ratings?
4. Does shipping type (Standard vs Express) influence how much customers spend on average?
5. How does average spending and total revenue differ between subscribers and non-subscribers?
6. Which products are most frequently purchased using promo codes, indicating high discount dependency?
7. What is the distribution of customers across New, Returning, and Loyal segments based on previous purchases?
8. Which products drive the highest sales volume within each product category?
9. Are repeat buyers (more than 5 previous purchases) more likely to be subscribers?
10. Which age group contributes the highest total revenue, and how does revenue vary across age segments?

## Business Recommendations
- Boost Subscriptions – Promote exclusive benefits for subscribers.
- Customer Loyalty Programs – Reward repeat buyers to move them into the “Loyal”
segment.
- Review Discount Policy – Balance sales boosts with margin control.
- Product Positioning – Highlight top-rated and best-selling products in campaigns.
- Targeted Marketing – Focus efforts on high-revenue age groups and express-shipping
users.
